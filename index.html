Key Fiqh Rules of Zakat

Nisab: The minimum wealth threshold a Muslim must possess before Zakat is due. Typically evaluated against 85g of gold or 595g of silver (the silver standard is more beneficial to the poor).

Hawl: Zakat is due once one full lunar year (approx. 354 days) has passed while maintaining wealth at or above Nisab.

Beneficiaries (Surah At-Tawbah 9:60): The poor, the needy, administrators of Zakat, hearts to be reconciled, freeing captives, those in debt, in the cause of Allah, and the stranded traveler.
